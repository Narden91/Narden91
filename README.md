## 👤 Profile

I am a **Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Eastern Finland (UEF), Center for Photonics Sciences**. My work combines machine learning with optical and THz measurements.

My current research covers **inverse-problem reconstruction, transfer-matrix-model optimisation, Raman analysis, spectral modelling, uncertainty quantification, and machine learning for THz data**.

I also work on **TEM image analysis, evolutionary optimisation, and reproducible research software**. During my PhD, I studied **handwriting-based markers for neurological screening**, using machine learning, Bayesian classifier fusion, deep learning, evolutionary computation, and explainable AI.
